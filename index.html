 3D MONSTER-TAMER ACTION ADVENTURE WITH REAL-TIME BEAT-EM-UP COMBAT THAT, AT ITS HEART, IS A DRAMATICALLY NEW TAKE ON THE TAMAGOTCHI EXPERIENCE.
